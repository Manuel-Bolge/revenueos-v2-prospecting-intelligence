## Before publishing

- Use only authorized fictional/test prospects and records.
- Blur or redact email addresses, contact names, Salesforce record IDs, private webhook URLs, credentials, header values, execution links, and customer information.
- Do not upload private CRM tables or secrets shown in browser/credential settings.
- Verify the screenshot actually comes from the working test. Do not use mockups as proof of execution.

Upload these files here when captured, or upload to this folder directly in GitHub. The main [V2 README](../README.md) links to this screenshot checklist.
